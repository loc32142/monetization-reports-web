Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Application: Monetization Reporting

This policy explains how Monetization Reporting, operated by the project administrator reachable at dangphuquang987@gmail.com, processes information for the Monetization Intelligence and Monetization Alert Analytics dashboards.

1. Information we access and collect

Google AdMob data: when an account operator authorizes the application, the backend uses the read-only admob.readonly permission to access account and app identifiers, app and ad unit names, mediation group identifiers and names, advertising sources, dates, countries, platforms, ad formats, estimated earnings, impressions, requests, matched requests, clicks and related reporting metrics. The application does not request Gmail messages, Drive files or an advertising end user's identity.

Authorization credentials: OAuth access and refresh credentials enable authorized background reporting. They are handled by the backend, not published in the report HTML.

Dashboard account information: the authentication provider processes the email address, password and session information used for viewer login. Hosting and authentication providers may also process IP addresses, request information and operational logs to deliver and protect the service.

2. Purpose and use

We use the information to authenticate viewers, retrieve authorized AdMob reports, calculate performance metrics, display trends and alerts, compare reporting periods, and maintain the reporting service. The Google permission is read-only: it is not used to modify AdMob accounts, advertising settings or mediation configurations.

Google API data is not sold or used for targeted advertising. Monetization Reporting's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

3. Storage, service providers and who can view reports

GitHub Actions runs the reporting jobs, GitHub Secrets stores backend credentials, GitHub Pages hosts the public frontend, and Supabase provides authentication and private snapshot storage. Information is processed by these providers to supply the service under their applicable terms and policies. Report snapshots and backend secrets are not intentionally included in the public frontend repository.

Current access model: the project operator and authenticated dashboard viewers can access shared reporting data. Any email can currently create a viewer account without email verification; there is no viewer email allowlist. Therefore dashboard access is not restricted to the Google users who authorized the AdMob accounts. Authorize an account only if you are permitted to share its reports through this access model.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to address security incidents. Service-provider processing and access by registered viewers described above are part of providing the dashboards.

4. Retention and browser storage

The backend currently retrieves a rolling 28-day reporting window and normally retains the current and previous compressed snapshots. Older snapshots are removed after successful publication and cleanup; failed cleanup may delay removal. Provider logs and backups can have separate retention periods.

OAuth credentials are retained while the authorized connection is maintained. Viewer accounts remain until removed by the operator. Contact us to request removal of information controlled by this application.

The browser stores authentication session information and a compressed snapshot cache in local browser storage/IndexedDB. The snapshot cache is scoped to the project, bucket and viewer account, and can be shared between the two dashboards on the same browser. Signing out clears the application's snapshot cache. You can also remove it through your browser's site-data settings. A different browser or device maintains separate storage. Any report files you export or download must be managed separately by you.

5. Revoking access and deletion requests

To stop future access to an authorized Google account, remove the application's access through Google Account connections. Revocation does not automatically delete reports already collected. For removal of deployed authorization credentials, stored reporting snapshots or a dashboard viewer account, contact dangphuquang987@gmail.com, describe the request and identify the affected account or app without sending passwords or tokens. We will review the request, verify the requester's authority and explain the action taken or any applicable retention requirement.

6. Security

The service uses HTTPS, backend secret storage and authenticated access to private snapshots. These safeguards do not guarantee absolute security. Avoid using shared browsers for sensitive reports, sign out when finished, and never send authorization tokens or passwords through support messages.

7. Changes and contact

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes to Google data use will be disclosed, with consent obtained where required. For privacy or support questions, contact dangphuquang987@gmail.com.

Tóm tắt: Công cụ đọc dữ liệu báo cáo AdMob để dựng hai dashboard; lưu trên Supabase và dùng GitHub để chạy tác vụ/host giao diện. Người đăng ký dashboard hiện có thể xem báo cáo chung. Bạn có thể thu hồi quyền tại tài khoản Google và liên hệ email trên để yêu cầu xóa dữ liệu hoặc tài khoản.
