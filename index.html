Understand your app monetization.

Monetization Reporting is a reporting tool for reviewing advertising performance from Google AdMob accounts connected by their account operators. It turns account-level advertising reports into daily trends, comparisons and diagnostics.

How Google data is used

Account operators grant the backend read-only access to AdMob reporting data using Google OAuth. The tool reads advertising metrics and reporting dimensions to build the dashboards. It does not use this permission to change campaigns, mediation settings or ad units, and does not request access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Scheduled jobs fetch completed reporting dates. Reports are updated after a complete snapshot is published; they are not a live, real-time feed. See the Privacy Policy for data storage, sharing, retention and revocation details.

Dashboard access

The public pages describe the tool. Dashboard data requires a separate email/password login. Under the current configuration, any email can register without email verification, and registered viewers can read the shared reports. Google account authorization and dashboard viewer registration are separate processes.
